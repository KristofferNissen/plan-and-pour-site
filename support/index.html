Support

Help with purchases, restore purchases, subscriptions, refunds, My Bar, Shopping, recipes, PDFs, and units.

Contact

Contact Plan & Pour support at planandpour.app@gmail.com.

Purchases

Monthly and annual premium access are App Store subscriptions. Premium Lifetime is a one-time purchase that does not expire. Purchases are billed by Apple.

Plan & Pour itself does not directly charge credit cards.

Restore Purchases

Use Restore purchases in Plan & Pour if a previously purchased premium entitlement is not showing.

Restoring does not create a new charge. It can be useful after reinstalling the app or changing devices.

Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Subscriptions

Subscription management and cancellation are handled through your Apple ID / App Store subscription settings.

Refunds

Refund requests for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Plan & Pour cannot promise that a refund will be granted.

To request a refund, use Apple's official request-a-refund process.

My Bar

My Bar helps you find drinks that match bottles you already own. My Bar does not subtract existing bottles from the Shopping list.

Shopping intentionally shows the total amount required for the full Party Plan. Compare that total with what you already have at home and shop for whatever you still need.

Shopping

Shopping quantities are estimates based on your Party Plan settings and selected drinks.

My Recipes and PDF export

My Recipes contains the drinks selected for your party. You can export a PDF containing ingredients and preparation instructions.

PDF sharing uses the iOS share sheet.

Units

In Party Plan, use the unit selector in the top-right corner to switch between cl and oz.
