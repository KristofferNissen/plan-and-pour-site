Privacy Policy

Plan & Pour is designed to keep party planning data on your device unless you choose to share it. This policy explains what the app stores locally, how purchases work, and which third-party services are involved.

Information stored on your device

Plan & Pour stores most app data locally on your device. This includes:

  • favorites
  • My Bar bottle selections
  • whether you have seen the intro screen
  • cached subscription entitlement status and last sync time

Party Plan data such as guest count, party length, selected drinks, drink mix, units, and recipe overrides is held in app state and is not uploaded to a Plan & Pour backend. Plan & Pour does not currently have a backend account system.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. Plan & Pour does not receive or store payment card or bank details.

Plan & Pour uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions, lifetime purchases, receipts, restore purchases, and premium entitlement status. RevenueCat may process purchase history and technical transaction information necessary for this purpose.

The app does not provide RevenueCat with your name, email address, phone number, or a custom logged-in account ID. RevenueCat uses an anonymous app user identifier. Purchase history is used for app functionality and analytics, is not linked by Plan & Pour to your real-world identity, and is not used for advertising tracking.

Third-party services

Plan & Pour uses these third-party services for app functionality:

  • Apple / App Store for processing purchases and subscriptions. See the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat for purchase and entitlement management. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Plan & Pour does not control the privacy practices of Apple or RevenueCat. Plan & Pour does not use advertising or attribution integrations.

Data we do not collect

Plan & Pour does not collect the following information through the app:

  • name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • precise or coarse location
  • contacts
  • photos, videos, camera data, microphone data, or audio recordings
  • health data or fitness data
  • browsing history
  • advertising identifiers, IDFA, or advertising data
  • crash analytics through a third-party crash SDK
  • behavioral analytics through a third-party analytics SDK

Tracking and advertising

Plan & Pour does not show ads, sell data, share personal data with advertising networks, or use cross-app or cross-site tracking.

PDF sharing

Recipe PDFs are generated locally on your device. A PDF only leaves the app if you explicitly choose to share or export it through the iOS share sheet. The destination you choose may have its own privacy practices.

Data retention and deletion

Deleting Plan & Pour from your device generally removes the locally stored app data described above. Purchase records and related transaction information may continue to be retained by Apple and RevenueCat in accordance with their policies and legal obligations.

Children's privacy and age suitability

Plan & Pour is an alcohol-related cocktail planning app and has been rated 18+ in App Store Connect. The app does not include independent age verification.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Plan & Pour changes how it stores data, handles purchases, or uses third-party services. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to provide, including your email address, in order to respond to your request.

For privacy questions, contact Plan & Pour at planandpour.app@gmail.com.
